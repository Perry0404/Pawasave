In one line

PawaSave is a live Nigerian savings-and-credit app on Base where deposits earn real, Treasury-bill-backed yield, and users borrow cNGN against those savings without breaking them. We are opening a dedicated liquidity pool to fund this loan book, and inviting you to supply cNGN into it for a secured, above-T-bill return.

The opportunity

Nigerian retail and SME credit is priced at 40–80% APR through informal lenders, while savers have no simple way to borrow against the value they already hold. PawaSave closes that gap: savings route into tokenized Nigerian Treasury bills and money-market funds (earning real yield in cNGN), and users borrow cNGN against those holdings — over-collateralized, far below the informal market.

How your capital is protected

  • Over-collateralization — no loan exceeds its collateral's lending value; T-bill collateral is stable and liquid.
  • Automated liquidation — an on-chain engine continuously values collateral and liquidates before a position goes underwater.
  • On-chain transparency — pool supply, utilization and collateral are verifiable on Base; you receive regular reporting.
  • Custody & audit — contract ownership behind a Gnosis Safe multisig; independent security audit completed and remediated.
  • Ring-fenced pools — customer savings stay invested in T-bills and are never lent; the loan book is funded only by this dedicated pool.

For cNGN / stablecoin ecosystem partners

Beyond return, this is a flagship utility use case for cNGN: real savings, real credit, and real Treasury-bill settlement, all denominated in cNGN and cycling on Base. It drives measurable cNGN demand, velocity and on-chain adoption among everyday Nigerians and SMEs — the regulated, productive utility the naira stablecoin needs to grow. We would gladly position PawaSave as a reference deployment and co-develop liquidity, distribution or ecosystem support.
