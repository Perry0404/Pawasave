01 — Why now

The Problem

Nigerians lose critical capital daily to persistent Naira devaluation — savings, credit and community finance are all broken at once.

Explosive Credit Cost

SMEs and traders pay predatory 40–80% interest on informal loans just to fund everyday working capital.

Savings You Can't Tap

To raise cash, Nigerians break their savings and investments — forfeiting the yield and compounding they were building.

No Naira Collateral Rails

There is no simple way to borrow against on-chain savings or assets in Naira, so billions in idle value can't unlock affordable credit.
